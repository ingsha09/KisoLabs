Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Limbu Keyboard mobile application (developed by Kiso Labs). We prioritize your privacy and are committed to protecting it through transparent data practices.

Summary: Limbu Keyboard operates completely offline. We do not collect, process, store, or transmit any personal data, keystrokes, passwords, or clipboard history.

1. Information Collection and Use

Limbu Keyboard does not collect any personal data or personal identifiable information (PII).

  • Keystrokes: Everything you type remains entirely local on your device. Your keystrokes are never logged, stored, or transmitted over any network.
  • Dictionary & Words: Smart word suggestions are generated using a static, locally stored dictionary file embedded within the application.
  • Clipboard History: If you use the built-in clipboard feature, copied text snippets are stored locally in your device's RAM/memory for your temporary session. This data is never sent to any external server.

2. Internet Permission & Network Access

Limbu Keyboard does not request or require network access permissions (Internet permission). Because the app lacks internet access, it is technically impossible for the application to send your data to us or any third party.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

The app does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics tracking tools (such as Google Analytics or Firebase), or advertising networks. We do not track your usage habits, device identifiers, or location.

4. System Permissions

Limbu Keyboard only uses standard Android system APIs strictly necessary for operation:

  • Input Method Service: Required by Android to act as a system-wide custom keyboard.
  • Vibration / Haptic Control: Used strictly to provide optional tactile feedback when pressing keys.
  • Audio Settings: Used strictly to play keypress click sounds if enabled in settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is completely safe for general audiences.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
