Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Thank you for using Limbu Dictionary. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application operates regarding user data and information storage.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Limbu Dictionary does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII). You can use this app fully without creating an account or providing any personal details.

2. Local Storage & Offline Caching

To provide a fast and reliable user experience, the app uses your device's internal storage (SharedPreferences) locally for the following purposes:

  • Dictionary Cache: The dictionary database is cached locally on your device so you can search and view definitions while offline.
  • Bookmarks: Words you choose to bookmark are saved strictly on your local device.

None of this cached data or bookmark history is uploaded to external servers or accessible by us.

3. Network Permissions

The app requires an internet connection solely for downloading or updating the initial dictionary dataset. Once synchronized, the application operates offline using locally saved data.

4. Third-Party Services

The application does not use third-party tracking, analytics tools, or advertising networks that harvest user data.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our application is safe for all ages. Because we do not collect any personal data, we do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
